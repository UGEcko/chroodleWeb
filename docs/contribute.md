# Contribute

Hey YOU! 
<br>

Do you have information you'd like to share all over the BeatSaber modding community? Chroodle may be the place you're looking for.

> Prior to Chroodle being out there, there was a bunch of data and information spread throughout various places like discord servers and git repositories. <br> Chroodle was created to change that, and to unify as much Heck-related information in one space as possible.


## How can I contribute?

If you have a request to add information to chroodle or just a small suggestion, reach out to me on discord: **ugecko**.

If you are unable to reach me on discord, please create an issue on the [ChroodleWeb Repo](https://github.com/UGEcko/chroodleWeb/issues) on github with the "integration request" tag attached.

<br>
Thank you for your interest in contributing to Chroodle!
